## 1. Photo Prompt Structure

Think through these questions before writing:
- How is the photo **composed**?
- What is the **emotional vibe**?
- How **close** are we to the subject? What **angle**?
- How much **depth of field**?
- How is the subject **lit**? Where from? How much light?
- **Artificial or natural** light? What color? What time of day?
- What **camera or lens**? Macro, telephoto, or wide angle?
- Where is it **shot**? Studio or real world?
- What **film or process**? Digital or film?
- What **year** was it taken?
- What **context** was this photo published in?

## 2. Camera Proximity & Framing

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Extreme close-up | Fills frame with a single detail |
| Close-up | Head and face |
| Medium shot / mid-shot / waist shot | Subject from waist up |
| Head & shoulders shot | Bust portrait |
| Long shot / wide shot / full shot | Full subject + surroundings |
| Extreme long shot / extreme wide shot | Subject tiny in landscape |
| X "in the distance" | Subject far from camera |

## 4. Camera Settings & Lenses

| Term | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Fast shutter speed / 1/1000 sec | Freezes motion, action photography |
| Slow shutter speed / long exposure / 1 sec shutter | Motion blur, light trails |
| Bokeh / shallow depth of field / out-of-focus background | Subject isolated, background blurred |
| Deep depth of field / f/22 / 35mm | All elements sharp |
| Macro lens / macro photo / Sigma 105mm F2.8 | Extreme close-up of small subjects |
| Telephoto lens / Sigma 500mm f/5 | Distant subjects, voyeuristic feel |
| Wide angle lens / 15mm | Fits more scene in frame |
| Fish-eye lens | Extreme distortion, bulging center |
| Tilt shift photography | Narrow strip in focus, miniature effect |
| Motion blur | Sense of speed and movement |

**Lens name hack:** Naming a specific lens (e.g., "Sigma 75mm") signals "professional photo" even without technical detail.

## 7. Film Stocks, Types & Processes

| Film Type | Character |
|-----------|-----------|
| Cinestill 800t | Cinematic, halation glow, night scenes |
| Kodachrome | Strong reds and greens, vintage |
| Autochrome | Queasy yellow-greens + hot pinks |
| Lomography | Oversaturated, hue-shifted |
| Polaroid / Instax | Soft focus, square, flash |
| Daguerreotype | Very early film, 1800s, aged |
| Disposable camera | Amateur, authentically candid |
| Cyanotype | Blue-and-white printing method |
| Black and white / Tri-X 400TX | Classic monochrome |
| Redscale | Heavy red tones |
| Infrared photography | Plants turn pink, eerie |
| Double exposure | Two subjects merged |
| CCTV / surveillance / dashcam | Gritty, black-and-white |
| Camera obscura / pinhole photography | Soft, dreamlike |
| Bleach bypass | Muted, desaturated (Saving Private Ryan look) |
| Instagram / Hipstamatic / 2015 | Faux-retro filtered |

**Film & TV hack:** Specify a real show/film + year to "steal the look":
- `from TV show Stranger Things (2016)`
- `from Blade Runner 2049 (2017)`
- `from action-adventure film "SHIVER ME TIMBERS!" (1973)` (imaginary)

## 8. Illustration Styles

### Monochrome / Analog
Ballpoint pen, Charcoal sketch, Pencil sketch, Pencil drawing (hyper-detailed),
Etching, Political cartoon, Coloring-in sheet, Stencil (Banksy), Woodcut, Field journal line art

### Color / Analog
Crayon, Child's drawing, Oil painting, Ukiyo-e, Chinese watercolor, Coloured pencil,
Pastels, Airbrush, Acrylic on canvas, Watercolor

### Digital
Alegria / corporate memphis, Collage / photocollage, Low poly, Vector art,
Layered paper, Sticker illustration, Storybook, Digital painting, Watercolor & pen,
Screen printing

### Instructional
Blueprint, Patent drawing, Botanical illustration, Cutaway, Mythological map,
IKEA manual, Scientific diagram, Instruction manual

### 3D & Textured
Isometric 3D, 3D render, Houdini 3D, Octane 3D, ZBrush, Maya, Cinema 4D, Blender,
Claymation (Aardman Animation), Pixel Art, Fabric pattern, Scratch art / foil art,
Perler beads, Tattoo, Felt pieces

### Character & Cartoon
Character reference sheet, Comic book art, Vintage Disney, Anime, 1990s Disney cel shading,
Pixar, Studio Ghibli, Hanna Barbera (1960s), Pixel Art

## 9. Art Movements & History

### Ancient / Medieval
Cave paintings (Lascaux, primitive), Ancient Egyptian mural, Roman mosaic,
Byzantine icon, Gilded codex (illuminated manuscript), Fayum portrait

## 13. Prompt Structure Templates

### Photography
```
A [vibe] photograph of [SUBJECT], [framing], [setting], [lighting], [lens/camera],
[film type/process], [year and publication context]
```

**Example:**
```
A vibrant photograph of [SUBJECT], wide shot, outdoors, sunset at golden hour,
wide-angle lens, soft focus, shot on iPhone 6, on Flickr in 2007
```

### Illustration / Art
```
[Medium/style] of [SUBJECT], [art movement or artist reference], [year],
[color palette], [mood], [detail level]
```

**Example:**
```
Oil painting of a young woman, Post-impressionist, 1895, muted earth tones,
melancholic, hyper-detailed brushwork, in the style of Vuillard
```

### 3D / Render
```
[SUBJECT], [render engine], [lighting], [camera angle], [style keywords],
[background], [quality boosters]
```

**Example:**
```
Cute rounded robot holding a warning sign, Octane render, high-key soft lighting,
low angle shot, shallow depth of field, blurred yellow background, hyper-detailed
```

### Film Still
```
Film still of [SUBJECT], [camera angle], [cinematography style], from [film/show] ([year])
```

**Example:**
```
Film still of two construction workers arguing, over-the-shoulder shot,
colourful cinematography from Amélie (2001)
```
